How It Works

Step 1: Install & Launch

Download and run Charles. It sets up as your system's HTTP proxy automatically.

Step 2: Configure Traffic

Point your browser or app to use Charles proxy (localhost:8888). All traffic routes through it.

Step 3: Record & Inspect

Start recording. View requests, responses, timelines, and payloads in an intuitive interface.

Step 4: Debug & Throttle

Edit, repeat, throttle, or breakpoint as needed. Export data for further analysis.
